A coffee shop with a game shelf (or several)

We have shelves of board games, cards, dice, chess sets, that kind of thing. People come in intending to grab coffee and somehow are still here two hours later, playing something with people they just met.

The shop has warm wood tables, mismatched chairs that somehow work together, plants in the windows, and a big community table in the middle. There are always cards and game pieces around.

One thing we like about tabletop games: people actually look at each other instead of another screen for a while.

You don’t need to be “a board-game person”

Some of our customers play games every week. Some haven’t played anything since Monopoly when they were ten. Both are fine.

Regular events

People ask about these all the time, so here they are.

  • Open Table Thursday

    Every Thursday · 6:30 to 9:30 PM

    Our biggest regular event. Bring your own games or grab something from our shelves. It’s fine to come alone.

  • Chess & Coffee

    Every Sunday · 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM

    It’s casual. You absolutely do not have to be good at chess.

  • Learn-a-Game Night

    First Wednesday of each month · 7:00 to 8:30 PM

    Someone from our staff picks a game and teaches it. Especially good if you like the idea of tabletop games but don’t know where to start.
