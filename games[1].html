New to tabletop games?

You don’t need to already be “a board-game person” to come here. Some of our customers play games every week. Some haven’t played anything since Monopoly when they were ten. Both are fine.

Learn-a-Game Night

First Wednesday of each month · 7:00 to 8:30 PM

Someone from our staff picks a game and teaches it. It’s especially good if you like the idea of tabletop games but don’t know where to start.

Open Table Thursday

Every Thursday · 6:30 to 9:30 PM

Bring your own games or grab something from our shelves. It’s fine to come alone.
